# Phase 5 — 90-Day Commercial Launch Plan (Radiant Advising Services)

Targets marked **[A]** are planning assumptions built from the benchmarks in `01-research-and-strategy.md`; they will be re-set after 30 days of real data. Budget lines are **proposals** — each needs your approval in the Control Center before any money is spent.

## North-star metric
**Qualified trade inquiries** (complete spec: grade, volume, Incoterm, destination) → **verified counterparties** → **closed tonnage / commission**.

## Budget guardrails (Financial Advisor)
- Total 90-day cash marketing spend proposed: **≤ US$3,000 [A]** excluding the CEO's time and the Claude API usage (set an Anthropic workspace limit, e.g. US$100/month [A]).
- Kill rule: any paid channel with zero qualified inquiries after 50% of its budget is paused.
- Payback logic: one recurring account at the illustrative US$6,250/month commission [E] repays the full 90-day budget in under one month — but only if it closes; track pipeline value, not leads alone.

## Compliance checklist (Legal — screen only, not legal advice)
- Privacy notice + consent for EU (GDPR) and KSA (PDPL) contacts before emailing; B2B legitimate-interest basis documented.
- No unsubstantiated superlatives ("largest", "100% guaranteed").
- Recovered-paper trade documentation: track EU waste-shipment rule changes affecting non-OECD importers by 2027.
- Confirm whether brokerage activity requires any commercial registration/licensing in KSA for the activity code — consult local counsel.
