## 6. How the business works

### 6.1 Logistics (Supply Chain & Logistics Expert)

- **No direct Jeddah/Dammam–Radès container service was found** [V, negative finding]. Expect transshipment through East-Med or Central-Med hubs (Port Said/Damietta, Malta, Piraeus, Gioia Tauro) [A: get quotes]. Jeddah's AE15 rotation already calls at Port Said and Damietta [V] ([Enterprise](https://enterpriseam.com/logistics/2023/06/13/maersk-adds-jeddah-islamic-port-to-two-key-shipping-lines-connecting-it-with-africa-and-europe/)).
- Ports: **Jeddah Islamic Port** (west coast factories) or **King Abdulaziz Port, Dammam** (Eastern Province petrochemicals) → **Radès** (Tunis), with **Sfax** for the southern plastics cluster.
- Tunisia moves over 90% of its trade by sea, and freight and insurance costs are rising with geopolitical tension [V] ([Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/economy/north-africa/soaring-global-shipping-costs-new-challenges-for-tunisias-economy-w8y1ll8w)). Radès congestion is a documented problem [V] ([Dispatch Risk](https://new.dispatchrisk.com/?p=17671)). **Red Sea and Suez risk affects Dammam-origin cargo more than Jeddah-origin cargo.**
- **Incoterms:** quote **CFR/CIF Radès** for Tunisian buyers (they expect landed prices). Use **FOB Jeddah/Dammam** only for buyers with their own forwarder. Avoid DDP: Tunisian import clearance should stay with the importer.
- **Action before day 30:** get 3 forwarder quotes for 1×40'HC Jeddah→Radès and Dammam→Radès (transit time, transshipment port, all-in price). That number decides which lines can compete.

### 6.2 Deal economics and commission (Financial & Commercial Analyst)

Radiant's published commission is 2.5% (docs/01) [V]. Illustrative containers follow. **Prices are assumptions; replace them with real quotes.**

| Line | Volume per 40' | Assumed price | Value per container [E] | Pool at 3% [E] |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PE film / sheet | ~22 t | US$1,300/t [A] | US$28,600 | US$858 |
| Paints / building chemicals | ~18 t | US$1,800/t [A] | US$32,400 | US$972 |
| Packaged olive oil (to KSA) | ~18 t | US$6,500/t [A] | US$117,000 | US$3,510 |

**Takeaway:** single containers don't pay for the work. **The value is in repeat orders.** One converter taking 4 containers a month of film earns about US$41 k a year in commission pool. A reliable olive-oil retail account taking 2 containers a month earns about US$84 k a year [E].

**Proposed structure** (open to negotiation):
1. **Success fee** of 2.5–3.5% of invoice value, paid by the seller. Fee drops to 1.5–2% on repeat orders after year 1.
2. **Split:** 50/50 when each party brings one side of the deal. **70/30 to the originator** when one party brings both sides and the other only executes.
3. **Market-entry package** for Saudi factories, sold by Export Growth and delivered jointly: buyer list, 5 qualified meetings, price benchmark and regulatory checklist. Indicative fee SAR 15–30 k per factory [A]. This covers costs before commissions arrive.
4. Commission is **payable only after the seller is paid**, and Radiant/Export Growth never hold buyer funds. This keeps both firms outside payment-service rules [K].
5. **Non-circumvention for 24 months** per introduced counterparty, covering all products.

## 9. Meeting plan (Executive Negotiation Advisor)

**Positioning (choose one and stick to it):**
- *If Radiant operates from Tunisia:* "Your execution partner on the ground in Tunisia and the Maghreb, with French/Arabic capability, buyer networks and import know-how."
- *If Radiant operates from Riyadh with a Tunisian team:* "A Riyadh-registered broker with a Tunisian buyer desk."

**Opening (2 minutes):** "Saudi–Tunisian trade reached TND 1.14 bn in 2025, and plastics account for about two-thirds of what Tunisia buys from the Kingdom. SABIC has its own channel. Mid-size Saudi factories don't. That's the gap we want to fill with you."

**Questions to ask**
1. How many factories do you represent, and in which sectors? Which already ship to North Africa?
2. Were any of the 34 companies on the July 2025 Saudi Exports Tunis mission your clients? What happened with their leads?
3. Do you work on retainer, success fee or both? Do you hold exclusivity with factories?
4. Do your clients use Saudi EXIM financing today?
5. Would you take Tunisian food and pharma exporters into the Saudi market?

**Likely objections and responses**
- *"We already have buyers in North Africa."* → "Then we can start with lines you don't cover, or support collections and regulation in Tunisia on your existing deals."

- *"Commission is too high."* → Offer the 1.5–2% repeat-order rate and the 70/30 originator split.

**Closing ask:** sign an MoU/NDA within 2 weeks, receive 5–8 factory profiles, and agree a date for a joint trip to Tunis.

**Bring:** this brief (printed, Arabic version if possible), a 1-page MoU term sheet built from §6.2 and §7, Radiant company registration documents, and 2–3 references.
